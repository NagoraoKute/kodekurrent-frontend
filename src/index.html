Schedule

Follow the route from Pallet Town to the Hall of Fame

  1. Day 1

  2. Day 1 · 9:00 AM

    ▶ Pallet Town

    Check-in & Opening Ceremony

    Check in at the desk, meet your fellow trainers and hear the rules of the route at the opening ceremony. Every great journey starts in Pallet Town.

  3. Day 1 · 11:00 AM

    ▶ Into the Tall Grass

    Hacking Begins

    The clock starts and the tall grass rustles. Time to build, because wild bugs can appear at any moment.

  4. Day 1 · 6:00 PM

    Mentor Check-in

    Mentors visit teams to review progress, answer questions and help you get past tough roadblocks.

  5. Day 1 · 11:59 PM

    Midnight Snacks & Mini-games

    Refuel with midnight snacks and take a break with mini-games. Even the best trainers need a Potion now and then.

  6. Day 2

  7. Day 2 · 11:00 AM

    ▶ The Elite Four

    Hacking Ends & Submissions

    Pencils down! Submit your project before the deadline, then get ready to face the judges.

  8. Day 2 · 2:00 PM

    ▶ Hall of Fame

    Pitches & Closing Ceremony

    Pitch your project to the judges and cheer on the finalists. The closing ceremony crowns the champions who enter the Hall of Fame.
